About GB Traveling Agency

Your trusted partner for Gilgit-Baltistan adventures since 2010

Our Story

Learn more about our journey and mission

GB Traveling Agency specializes in showcasing the breathtaking beauty of Gilgit-Baltistan, Pakistan. Founded in 2010, we've been dedicated to providing unforgettable travel experiences across the stunning mountain regions of Hunza Valley, Skardu, Deosai, and beyond. Our expert guides and local knowledge ensure every journey is memorable and safe.

Why Choose GB Traveling for Gilgit-Baltistan?

With over 15 years of experience in Gilgit-Baltistan tourism, we know every hidden gem from Attabad Lake to Khunjerab Pass. Our team combines professional expertise with deep local knowledge to create authentic, immersive travel experiences.

Our Mission

To showcase the majestic beauty of Gilgit-Baltistan to travelers worldwide while preserving its pristine nature and supporting local communities. We believe in responsible tourism that benefits both travelers and destinations.
